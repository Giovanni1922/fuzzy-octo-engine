Nossa história

Feito com amor.

I grew up one block from the calçadão. Every summer evening smelled exactly like this — coconut sunscreen, sea salt, grilled corn from the barraca on the corner, and the particular sweetness of air when the sun touches the water just before it disappears.

When I moved to São Paulo for work, I missed it so much it hurt. So I started making candles in my kitchen. Just to remember.

Casa Bonita is not a big brand. It's just me, my hands, and everything I love about home. I hope when you light this, you feel it too.

— Ana, Rio de Janeiro

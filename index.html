Our story

Made with
real love.

I grew up one block from the beach. Every summer evening smelled exactly like this — coconut, salt, grilled corn from the corner stand, and something in the air that made you feel like nothing bad could ever happen.

When I left Rio, I missed it more than I expected. So I started making candles in my kitchen — just to remember. Now I make them for everyone who's ever fallen in love with Brazil and left a little piece of themselves there.

— Ana, Rio de Janeiro

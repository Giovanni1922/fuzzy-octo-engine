Born in Rio.

Ana began pouring candles from her apartment overlooking Copacabana Beach, inspired by the scents that defined her summers growing up — sunscreen, warm coconut, salt air, and evening florals. Each Casa Bonita candle is handpoured in small batches in Rio de Janeiro, using a coconut wax blend chosen for its clean, slow burn and silky texture.
